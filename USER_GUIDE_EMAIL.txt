We've streamlined two important tools in OpsIQ to help you work more efficiently. Here's how to use them:

📦 INVENTORY AUDITOR (Simplified 3-Step Process)

The Inventory Auditor helps verify shelf inventory against Famous inventory records.

How to use:

1. Upload – Start on the Inventory Auditor page and upload your Famous inventory CSV report

2. Scan – The system will guide you through each lane:
   - Scan the lane placard to confirm the location
   - Scan each pallet in that lane (the system will automatically switch to pallet scan mode)
   - Mark the lane complete once all pallets are scanned

3. Audit – The system automatically runs the audit when you've completed all lanes, showing any discrepancies between scanned quantities and the Famous report

The process displays exactly what's needed at each step, so you'll always know what to do next.

---

📚 DOCK CHECKER HISTORY (Search & Review Forms)

The Dock Checker History lets you search and review all inbound and outbound dock checker forms submitted by your team.

How to use:

1. Go to Dock Checker History from the home screen

2. Use the filters to narrow results:
   - Date Range – Select start and end dates (defaults to last 7 days)
   - Form Type – Choose "Inbound," "Outbound," or "All Forms"
   - Search – Find forms by reference number, company, checker name, or notes

3. Click "Details" on any form to expand and see:
   - Who submitted it and when
   - Door ID, check-in ID, pallets offloaded/loaded
   - QC issues or damage flags
   - All attached photos from the inspection

This makes it easy to verify work, track team activity, and resolve any questions about past shipments.
